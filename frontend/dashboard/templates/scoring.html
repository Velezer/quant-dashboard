THE SCORING GUIDE

Understand every point.

A signal score describes the strength of a research setup. Here’s how the points add up, what each indicator contributes, and when a recorded score is still useful.

02 / THE INGREDIENTS

Four indicators. Four scores.

NWE, DSS Bressert, relative volume, and the delta proxy each have a separate /10 score and 25% maximum weight.

010–10 pts · 25%

Nadaraya–Watson Envelope

How close is price to an envelope band, or has it crossed that band? The lower-band setup points LONG; the upper-band setup points SHORT.

Closer to the selected band → more points

A registered cross earns 10. Near-band strength falls as the close moves away from its nearest band. Opposing NWE support adds zero to a DSS-led setup.

020–10 pts · 25%

DSS Bressert

Is the oscillator crossing its trigger or approaching a cross in the setup’s direction? The exact points depend on which model leads the setup.

Cross or near-cross → directional evidence

A DSS-led cross earns 10. NWE-led models instead score supporting DSS cross/near-cross and recent extreme-turn evidence.

030–10 pts · 25%

Relative volume

Compare the current bar’s volume with the average of its preceding 20 bars, for the same instrument, timeframe, and source.

1× or less → 0 · 1.5× → 5 · 2× or more → 10

Points count only if the candle direction agrees with the signal: rising for LONG, falling for SHORT. A neutral or opposing candle contributes zero.

040–10 pts · 25%

Estimated volume delta

Estimate candle pressure from where the close sits within its high–low range. A close near the high supports LONG; near the low supports SHORT.

More aligned close location → more points

This OHLCV proxy is not aggressor-side buy/sell trade delta. The volume factors use related observations, so they are not independent trade-flow measurements.

Show the formulas and model differences

NWE band proximity

10 × clamp(1 − distance / (0.5 × band width), 0, 1)

Band width is upper minus lower; distance is from the close to the nearest band. An equidistant midpoint has no direction. Signal scoring uses causal endpoint bands; the chart’s repainting overlay is a separate view.

DSS-led near-cross

10 × (1 − |DSS − trigger| / 5)

Applies to a qualifying approach within a 5-point oscillator spread. A confirmed directional cross earns 10. Being nearby alone is not enough: the model must classify a directional approach.

DSS support in an NWE-led model

10 × (cross/near points + extreme-turn points) / 6

Directional cross: 3 raw points; near-cross: 2. A recent same-direction extreme with a current turn adds 3. The six-point maximum is normalized to 10; missing support adds zero.

Relative volume

10 × clamp(relative volume − 1, 0, 1)

Relative volume = current volume ÷ the average of the previous 20 bars. The candle must agree with the signal. Missing, non-positive, or invalid volumes make this factor unavailable.

Estimated OHLCV delta

close location = clamp((2C − H − L) / (H − L), −1, 1)points = 10 × max(direction × close location, 0)

C, H, and L are close, high, and low; direction is +1 for LONG or −1 for SHORT. Required prices and volume must be valid, with positive volume and a positive high–low range.

04 / READ THE LABEL TOO

The same number can mean different things.

Check the state beside a score before comparing setups. A refresh does not guarantee a qualifying signal.

CURRENT · CLOSED BAR

Fresh and qualifying

The model qualifies on a completed bar, with a fresh same-source price. The score belongs to that model and timeframe.

PROVISIONAL

The bar is still forming

Stockbit retained NWE/DSS scores can use a forming bar. Its score and direction can change before the bar closes. Crypto NWE/DSS signals use completed bars.

NOT QUALIFYING · 0

A real zero

The latest fresh bar no longer qualifies. A retained Stockbit model row keeps a zero state; the screener’s minimum-score filter can hide it.

STALE · HELD VALUE

Last recorded score

Fresh inputs are missing or the scan is out of date. Keep the recorded number and its scale; it is not a current entry signal. Use Refresh signals on the screener to retry.

FACTOR UNAVAILABLE

Missing is not agreement

Invalid or missing inputs contribute zero for that factor and remain labeled unavailable. An unavailable total is shown as a dash, rather than an invented zero.

LEGACY MODEL

Keep its original scale

An older model retains its recorded /10 or /20 basis. It is not converted into the four-indicator display. Match the model version before comparing scores.

Two separate clocks. The score bar is checked against its own signal timeframe. The current price has its own freshness check, usually from 15-minute candles. Neither a new scan nor a weekly score makes an old price fresh.

05 / MATCH THE MODEL

Other models keep their own basis.

The model name and recorded score basis tell you which calculation applies.

STRATEGY + CONFLUENCE · /10

A weighted blend

Models routed through the shared confirmation scorer combine strategy strength with a normalized confluence score.

0.70 × strategy + 0.30 × confluence

Both components are on a 0–10 scale. Confluence starts with up to 11 raw points, then is normalized to 10.

What contributes to confluence?
  • DSS cross: 3 points; near-cross: 2.
  • Recent directional DSS extreme and current turn: 3.
  • NWE outer-band body touch: 3; wick touch: 2.
  • Near-line close: 1; directional band reclaim: 1.

A directional NWE touch and DSS extreme are eligibility conditions. The signal candle must close inside the envelope; points alone do not bypass those conditions.

TOKOCRYPTO · VOTE GATE

At least 2 of 4 vote LONG

The Tokocrypto voting strategy checks EMA 20/50, RSI, range breakout, and relative volume. It requires at least two LONG votes and more LONG than SHORT votes; a 2–2 tie does not qualify.

Neutral or unavailable indicators do not vote. The strategy emits LONG signals only, then applies the confirmation blend described alongside.

This vote count is an eligibility gate, not an indicator-point subtotal. Separate Tokocrypto NWE/DSS models show their own four separate /10 indicators. Crypto spot and tokenized stocks remain distinct categories.

Compare like with like. Use the same model version, app, product/category, timeframe, and data-source basis. Proxy candles remain research data; a higher score does not turn them into venue-native execution evidence.
