    LIVE POSITION SIZING

    Autocorrelation sizing is available for research.

    The 4h risk study compares fixed and autocorrelation-adjusted size on validation and untouched holdout data.

    Research only
    Read the backtest protocol and limits

    Match the live entry model

    The study calculates all six signed indicators on each eligible completed bar and derives direction from their weighted sum. Entry is at the next bar’s open. Markov contributes +10 for a bullish regime, −10 for a bearish regime, and zero for a neutral regime. Lag-one autocorrelation adds a causal directional AR(1) forecast score from same-timeframe returns through that bar; it needs at least 30 adjacent pairs and nonzero variance. Each fixed component is worth at most 10 points. A single position is evaluated at a time.

    Fit weights, then select rules

    A predeclared grid tests 165 nonnegative weight vectors for the first four indicators in 5-point steps that sum to 40; Markov and lag-one autocorrelation each have a fixed 10-point weight, for a signed total from −60 to +60. Eligibility floors use its absolute magnitude. Training fits weights by after-fee win rate. Validation selects the 4h ATR stop, fee-adjusted target-to-stop R multiple, setup-strength floor, and maximum hold; the untouched chronological holdout evaluates the frozen rule once. The target price solves the configured buy/sell fee model so planned net target reward is the selected multiple of planned net stop loss. The performance target is a 50% win rate after configured fees and its 95% Wilson lower bound, plus minimum sample and history checks. Payoff and return are reported but are not pass/fail targets. Profile promotion also checks app/product coverage and source quality; research metrics never filter live signals. The bracket-search qualification is specific to 4h; other timeframe reports remain available for comparison.

    Respect source and costs

    Reports retain their source family. Weight fitting is disabled when transaction fees are unknown. Returns subtract configured transaction fees; spread, slippage, impact, funding and borrow are not modeled, so results are after configured fees but not every execution cost. A stop gap can worsen realized R:R. The bracket simulator assumes the stop fills first if both stop and target touch in one candle.

    Read limits are not retention limits

    The 1,000-day history span is a qualification minimum, not a maximum lookback or storage limit. Each report uses every completed candle available in its sample up to the separately disclosed read cap; older candles may remain stored when that cap is reached.

    Cohort profiles pool one exact vector across registered instruments and evaluate app/product slices separately. Profile versions are immutable; a scan pins one snapshot and applies new versions on the next scan. Repeatedly tuning after reading the holdout contaminates it. A passing historical sample is not a guarantee of future results.
