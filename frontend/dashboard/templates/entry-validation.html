    NEXT STAGE / SIZING

    Entry evidence is the prerequisite.

    A sizing model is not built on a score alone.

    Blocked
    Read the backtest protocol and limits

    Match the live entry model

    The study uses the actual NWE-led or DSS-led signal and all five score components at the completed signal bar. Entry is at the next bar’s open. Markov adds 10 points only for a directional regime aligned with the signal; it contributes zero for opposing, neutral, or unavailable states. A single position is evaluated at a time.

    Fit weights, then select rules

    A predeclared grid tests 165 nonnegative weight vectors for the first four indicators in 5-point steps that sum to 40; the fifth Markov factor has a fixed 10-point weight, for a total scale of /50. Training fits weights by after-fee win rate. Validation selects the 4h ATR stop, fee-adjusted target-to-stop R multiple, score floor, and maximum hold; the untouched chronological holdout evaluates the frozen rule once. The target price solves the configured buy/sell fee model so planned net target reward is the selected multiple of planned net stop loss. The performance target is a 50% win rate after configured fees and its 95% Wilson lower bound, plus minimum sample and history checks. Payoff and return are reported but are not pass/fail targets. Profile promotion also checks app/product coverage and source quality; research metrics never filter live signals. The bracket-search qualification is specific to 4h; other timeframe reports remain available for comparison.

    Respect source and costs

    Reports retain their source family. Weight fitting is disabled when transaction fees are unknown. Returns subtract configured transaction fees; spread, slippage, impact, funding and borrow are not modeled, so results are after configured fees but not every execution cost. A stop gap can worsen realized R:R. The bracket simulator assumes the stop fills first if both stop and target touch in one candle.

    Read limits are not retention limits

    The 1,000-day history span is a qualification minimum, not a maximum lookback or storage limit. Each report uses every completed candle available in its sample up to the separately disclosed read cap; older candles may remain stored when that cap is reached.

    Cohort profiles pool one exact vector across registered instruments and evaluate app/product slices separately. Profile versions are immutable; a scan pins one snapshot and applies new versions on the next scan. Repeatedly tuning after reading the holdout contaminates it. A passing historical sample is not a guarantee of future results.
