    NEXT STAGE / SIZING

    Entry evidence is the prerequisite.

    A sizing model is not built on a score alone.

    Blocked
    Read the backtest protocol and limits

    Match the live entry model

    The study uses the actual NWE-led or DSS-led signal and its four indicator contributions at the completed signal bar. Entry is at the next bar’s open. A single position is evaluated at a time.

    Compare exit rules without holdout tuning

    Validation compares fixed 4, 8, and 12 bar exits with exits on the first qualifying opposite entry signal, each capped at the same maximum hold. The opposite signal closes the open position and does not reverse into a new one. The untouched holdout evaluates the selected rule once.

    Respect source and costs

    Research-proxy history stays labeled. Known transaction fees are separate from missing slippage, impact, funding, borrow, and other execution costs. Gross-only research cannot become validated execution evidence.

    Read limits are not retention limits

    Each evaluation reads a bounded sample. Earlier candles may remain in SQLite even when the read limit is reached. Candle storage has no application maximum age; the evidence minimum is not a storage cap.

    Repeatedly tuning a model after reading its holdout contaminates that holdout. Treat these results as research observations, preserve protocol and source identity, and use genuinely new data for subsequent independent validation.
